## 1. Quick start and navigation

The GSF Hub brings learning, practical tools, project stories, monitoring data, experts, and peer exchange into one platform. Most public content can be browsed without an account; learning enrollment, profile changes, and forum participation require sign-in.


![GSF Hub homepage and primary navigation on the local test site.](/img/guides/end-01-home.png)

*Figure 1. GSF Hub homepage and primary navigation on the local test site.*

### Use the main menu

| Menu | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Learn | Browse courses, enroll, launch course content, view progress, submit feedback, and access certificates. |
| Resources | Search and filter templates, toolkits, reports, videos, and field-ready materials. |
| Case Studies | Read outcome stories and filter them by type, focus area, and country or region. |
| Data Centre | View project, participation, geographic, indicator, and Ecoequity reporting. |
| Directory | Find practitioners and partner organizations by name, profile text, and country. |
| Forum | Browse community spaces; sign in to create topics, reply, and subscribe. |

### Search from the homepage

1. Open the homepage.
1. Enter a keyword in Search the site.
1. Select Search, then open the most relevant result.
**Tip.** Use a short, specific phrase such as gender toolkit, Saint Lucia, or project management. Use each section's filters when you already know the content type.

## 2. Create an account, sign in, and manage your profile

Create one account and use it for learning, forum participation, expert-roster activity, and profile settings.

### Create an account


![Member registration form.](/img/guides/end-10-register.png)

*Figure 2. Member registration form.*

1. Select Sign in in the site header, then select Register.
1. Enter a username, first name, last name, email address, and a strong password.
1. Complete the optional profile information: gender, age, organization type, country, and stakeholder groups.
1. Select Register. Follow any activation or approval instructions sent by email.
1. Return to Sign in and use your username or email address and password.
**Privacy reminder.** Only provide profile and stakeholder information you are comfortable storing in your GSF Hub account. Review Privacy settings from your Account page after signing in.

### Sign in or reset your password


![Member sign-in page, including registration and password-reset links.](/img/guides/end-09-sign-in.png)

*Figure 3. Member sign-in page, including registration and password-reset links.*

- Use Forgot your password? if you cannot sign in.
- Use Stay logged in only on a private device.
- If a protected page sent you to Sign in, the site should return you to that page after successful login.
### Use your Account page


![Signed-in member Account page and Quick Access cards.](/img/guides/end-11-account.png)

*Figure 4. Signed-in member Account page and Quick Access cards.*

- Update your first name, last name, or email address under Account.
- Use Change Password to replace your password.
- Use Privacy to control directory visibility where available.
- Use the Quick Access cards to open Learn, the Expert Roster portal, or the Expert Directory.
**Do not share accounts.** Course progress, feedback, certificates, subscriptions, and forum activity are tied to the signed-in account.

## 3. Use Learn and earn a certificate

The Learn area combines a course catalogue with a personal progress dashboard.


![Signed-in Learn catalogue with Enroll buttons and the learner dashboard.](/img/guides/end-12-learning-signed-in.png)

*Figure 5. Signed-in Learn catalogue with Enroll buttons and the learner dashboard.*

### Enroll and start a course

1. Open Learn and choose a course title.
1. Select Enroll. The page changes from Enroll to Status: Enrolled.
1. Open or continue the embedded course content.
1. Complete every required screen, activity, and assessment. Course progress is saved to your account.
1. Return to Learn to review your course status.

![Enrolled course page showing course status and the feedback step.](/img/guides/end-16-course-enrolled.png)

*Figure 6. Enrolled course page showing course status and the feedback step.*

### Complete feedback and access the certificate

- The course package must report Completed or Passed before feedback becomes available.
- After completion, select Submit feedback survey and submit the required responses.
- The certificate is issued only after verified course completion and feedback.
- Use Print or Save PDF on the certificate card to retain a copy.
**If progress does not update.** Keep the course page open while studying, allow the final completion screen to finish saving, then refresh the course page. If the problem remains, record the course name, date, and last completed section before contacting support.

## 4. Find resources and case studies

### Search the Resource Library


![Resource Library search and filters.](/img/guides/end-04-resources.png)

*Figure 7. Resource Library search and filters.*

1. Open Resources.
1. Enter a keyword, or choose a resource type, topic, and country or region.
1. Select Apply filters.
1. Open a resource card to review its description and download or external link.
1. Clear the fields or use Reset when you want to start a new search.
### Browse case studies


![Case Studies page with search and filter controls.](/img/guides/end-05-case-studies.png)

*Figure 8. Case Studies page with search and filter controls.*

- Search by title or keyword.
- Narrow results by case study type, focus area, and country or region.
- Open a result to review the context, actions, outcomes, partners, and reusable lessons.
## 5. Explore the Data Centre and Expert Directory

### Read the Data Centre


![Public Data Centre storytelling dashboard.](/img/guides/end-06-data-centre.png)

*Figure 9. Public Data Centre storytelling dashboard.*

- Use summary cards for the current number of projects, territories, participants, and course completions.
- Scroll through participation, geographic, project-status, indicator, and Ecoequity sections.
- Treat dashboard figures as the current published view; contact GSF staff if a figure needs clarification.
### Find an expert


![Expert Directory search and country filter.](/img/guides/end-07-directory.png)

*Figure 10. Expert Directory search and country filter.*

1. Open Directory.
1. Search by name, organization, role, or profile text.
1. Choose a country when you need a regional match.
1. Open a profile to review public information and areas of expertise.
## 6. Join forum discussions


![Signed-in Forum page with community spaces and activity counts.](/img/guides/end-14-forum-signed-in.png)

*Figure 11. Signed-in Forum page with community spaces and activity counts.*

### Create a topic

1. Sign in and open Forum.
1. Choose the forum space that best matches your question or update.
1. Scroll to Create New Topic.
1. Enter a clear title, write the topic, and add relevant tags.
1. Optionally select Notify me of follow-up replies via email.
1. Select Submit once. Your topic appears in that forum.

![New-topic form in a community forum.](/img/guides/end-15-forum-new-topic.png)

*Figure 12. New-topic form in a community forum.*

### Reply and subscribe

- Open a topic and use the reply form to contribute.
- Use Subscribe on a forum or topic when you want email updates.
- Keep posts relevant, respectful, and free of confidential personal or project information.
**Forum etiquette.** Use descriptive titles, acknowledge sources, and avoid posting the same question in multiple spaces.

## 7. Subscribe to updates

The Mailing List form appears in the site footer.

1. Scroll to Mailing List.
1. Enter your email address.
1. Select Subscribe.
1. Complete any confirmation step sent by email.
**Subscription is separate from membership.** A GSF Hub account does not automatically subscribe you to newsletters, and a newsletter subscription does not create a member account.

## 8. Troubleshooting and quick reference

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Cannot sign in | Check username or email, use Forgot your password?, and check junk mail for the reset message. |
| No course content | Confirm you are signed in and enrolled, then refresh the course page. |
| Course still in progress | Finish the course's final screen and allow it to save before refreshing. |
| Feedback is locked | The course must first report Completed or Passed. |
| No certificate | Complete the course, submit feedback, then refresh and open the certificate card. |
| No filter results | Clear one or more filters, shorten the keyword, or reset the form. |
| Cannot post in Forum | Confirm you are signed in and that your account has forum participation access. |

### Before contacting support

- Record the page or course title and the time the problem occurred.
- Take a screenshot of the visible message without exposing your password.
- Note the browser and device you were using.
